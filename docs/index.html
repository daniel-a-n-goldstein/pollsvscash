How the wedge is computed

Market prices are prediction-market prices (Polymarket and Kalshi). Poll-implied probabilities convert aggregated polling into win probabilities via a simple historical-error model. Wedge = market − polls, in percentage points. A positive wedge means the money is more optimistic than the survey data; it may reflect real information the polls miss — or pure hope. Wedges near 0% or 100% are inflated by the favorite-longshot bias (markets rarely price above ~90¢), and races marked "thin market" trade on little money, so read those wedges loosely. Chamber-control "poll" numbers come from a simple seat-by-seat simulation of current race polling, not a single survey. Chart history before 2026-07-12 is seeded from the markets' reported 1-day/1-week/1-month price changes; poll benchmarks are held flat over seeded days. Full details: methods & assumptions. This is a data project, not investment or voting advice.
