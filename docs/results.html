Brier score: the squared gap between a probability and the outcome (0 or 1), averaged across races. Lower is better; a coin flip scores 0.25. "Closer" is the simpler version: whose Nov 2 number was nearer the outcome. Scoring rules are on the methods page. Data: data.json. A project by Daniel A. N. Goldstein; code on GitHub.
