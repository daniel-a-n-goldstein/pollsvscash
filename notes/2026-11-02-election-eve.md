# Election eve update — November 2, 2026

*Posted on LinkedIn. Numbers are as of that morning's board; the scored numbers are the last point recorded on November 2.*

Election eve update on The Hope Index

As of this morning, the money is more bullish on Democrats than the polls in 8 of the 11 races I'm tracking. The money is betting on a bigger blue wave than the pollsters see.

Michigan is the most interesting case. The latest polls, all fielded before the weekend, have El-Sayed up 5 points. Then Rep. Haley Stevens (D) endorsed Rogers (R) on Sunday and the market dropped 8 points within hours. It has recovered some since, but the money now has El-Sayed about 4 points below where the polls put him. The polling average is unlikely to move much before tomorrow, so my poll tracker is effectively set. This is a nice test of the Hope Gap: whether the bettors saw something or overreacted.

After the election, a new scorecard will say which was closer, the polls or the money: https://daniel-a-n-goldstein.github.io/pollsvscash/results.html

A few of the other big gaps:

- Alaska: the market gives Peltola 74% against a polling average that has her up by about one point (60% once converted to a win probability).
- Senate control: the market sits 9 points above Decision Desk HQ's forecast.
- Texas: Talarico at 76% on the markets vs 68% from the polls.
